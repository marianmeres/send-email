Low-level, dependency-light "send an email" path, usable as a library and a CLI
(npm, with a `send-email` bin; JSR for Deno). Provides a provider-agnostic transport
interface, a nodemailer SMTP transport (with attachments and a real connect+auth
verify()), a mock transport, a one-shot send() helper, a pure resolveSmtpOptions()
env-shape → options mapper, and a first-class CLI with send/verify subcommands.
Not an email system — no queueing, persistence, templating, or retries.
